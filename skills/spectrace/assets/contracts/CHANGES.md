# Changes

> Owns: changes of direction: what was replaced, when and why · Not here: release notes or commit history (git), plans (planning/), standing rules (the other contracts)

A decision recorded here is revisable. It explains why we moved; it does not forbid moving again.

Entry format: `### YYYY-MM-DD — Title`, then **What changed**, **Why** and **Replaces** (a struck spec item such as `~~D1~~ → D2`, or the contract section that changed). Newest first.
